[R]overTech is a branch of [Polispace](https://polispace.it/), a student association of Milan Polytechnic University. We are currently working on a rover for the [ERC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Rover_Challenge) competition.

I'm responsible for the control stack of both locomotion and robot arm. This is carried out using the ROS 2 framework, specifically ros2_control. Wittenstein's Symco servo actuators are interfaced via EtherCAT with an Nvidia Jetson Nano board which runs Isaac ROS.

You can have a look at our rover in the video above.
